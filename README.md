## English

### 🎨 What is Banana Editor?

Banana Editor is a **free, professional AI-powered photo editing platform** powered by Google's **Gemini 2.5 Flash (Nano Banana 2)** model. Edit your photos with simple text commands - no complex software or design skills needed.

**🎁 Get Started Free:**
- ✅ **3 free credits** instantly upon signup
- ✅ **Earn up to 100+ credits** through daily check-ins
- ✅ **No credit card required**
- ✅ **Instant results** in seconds

### ✨ Key Features

🔒 **Identity-Safe Editing**
- Lock faces, hairstyles, and brand assets while editing other details
- Maintain subject consistency across multiple edits
- Perfect for portraits, products, and brand content

🎯 **Granular Control**
- Change backgrounds without reshoots
- Swap outfits, props, and accessories
- Adjust lighting and time of day
- Add or remove objects with precision

🚀 **Production Ready**
- 20+ curated prompt templates for portraits, e-commerce, interiors, and VFX
- Automated quality restore after multi-turn edits
- SynthID watermark detection and artifact removal
- High-resolution exports for campaigns and lookbooks

🌍 **Multi-Language Support**
- Full support for English, Chinese (简体中文), and Korean (한국어)
- Localized interface and documentation

### 🎯 Perfect For

- **Content Creators** - Social media posts, visual storytelling, mood boards
- **E-commerce** - Product photography, background removal, style consistency
- **Fashion & Virtual Try-On** - Outfit changes, accessory swaps, wardrobe previews
- **Film & Advertising** - Scene composition, lighting adjustments, VFX previz
- **Interior Design** - Furniture replacement, material swaps, style guidance
